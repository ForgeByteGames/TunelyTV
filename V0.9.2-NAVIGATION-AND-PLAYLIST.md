# TunelyTV v0.9.2 – Simplified navigation & remembered playlists

- On signed-in Live TV, the most recently selected **saved** M3U playlist is restored automatically.
- Playlist preference is saved per authenticated account in SQLite using `/api/player/preferences`. Nothing sensitive (stream URL, credentials) is saved in that preference table.
- On first visit, the first available saved playlist is selected; if a saved playlist was deleted, another available playlist is selected.
- Loading a playlist **does not automatically start playback** (browser autoplay restrictions and user control).
- Session-imported local files cannot survive a page refresh, so those selections are not remembered.
- The dashboard overview is reduced to Watch Live, Playlists, and Devices; detailed pairing/setup remains in the other pages.
- Live TV reduces nonessential header, decorative material, redundant legal notices, and large spacing; the player and channel list take priority.
- On mobile, tap **Channels** to open a slide-out channel browser without scrolling past the guide; selecting a channel closes the panel and returns to the player.
- No changes to encryption keys, existing accounts, prices, sources, pairing, or streaming proxy policy.

## Deploy to existing Railway app

1. Download the complete ZIP and extract it. Upload its *contents* to the root of the existing ForgeByteGames/TunelyTV GitHub repository, replacing files with matching names.
2. Commit, let Railway redeploy the existing service. **Do not create a second Railway service or replace the existing persistent volume.**
3. Keep the existing `TUNELY_DB`, `TUNELY_FERNET_KEY`, `TUNELY_SIGNUP_CODE` values and volume at `/app/instance` unchanged.
4. Open Watch Live, choose playlist B, refresh, confirm B is selected. Sign out and back in, confirm B remains selected. Change to another playlist and repeat.
5. Open Overview and confirm the compact home; check mobile and desktop sizing.
6. Multi-View remains subject to private-preview entitlement configuration and browser/provider playback limitations; this update does not resolve a deployed `preview:false` flag.

**Not deployed by ChatGPT.** Please verify the application in Railway after you commit the code.
