# v0.9.3 — TV guide-first navigation

## Design changes

- Immersive channel/program guide uses the viewport instead of sitting below video. Fixed 54px title row, compact selected-program summary, inline search/playlist/filter/timeline actions, fixed footer, and scrolling grid.
- Sticky channel names and timeline; responsive grid rows approximate 56–62 pixels, allowing 7+ rows at typical mobile and desktop heights. Vertical scroll remains inside the guide, not the page. Horizontal swipe moves through schedule time.
- The selected program's title, description, real start/end times and time-progress appear only if the user imported XMLTV metadata. No artwork or titles are fabricated.
- Search, Favorites, Earlier, Now, Later, playlist switching, and Guide/Watch TV navigation are available without leaving the screen. Existing M3U parser, direct video connections and XMLTV guides remain unchanged.
- Guide launches from the Live TV header or toolbar, keyboard G; Esc or Watch TV closes it. It appears by default for new users after the last-saved playlist is restored; individual browsers remember if Watch TV was chosen instead.
- The normal player and Multi-View remain separate modes. Selecting a guide cell attempts to play that source; whether playback works depends on permission, HTTPS/CORS and codecs. Existing backend trial/paid-plan enforcement is still incomplete.

## Usability and privacy

All demo screenshots use fictitious channel labels and synthetic XMLTV schedule data, which are **not shipped in the app**. No bundled IPTV channels, external program artwork, third-party logos or playback proxy. This version is appropriate for private staging, not public paid release.
