# TunelyTV v0.10.0 — Playlist Movies & Series Libraries

Personal, authorized Movies and TV Series come from the account's saved playlist sources; they are separate from public Where-to-Watch discovery. Uploaded M3U files and accessible authorized M3U URLs may be classified as Live TV, Movies/VOD, Series/Episodes, or Automatic. Source interpretation is stored encrypted and can be updated without re-adding the source.

- A standalone licensed film HLS URL can be designated a Movies source.
- Ambiguous rows (e.g. broadcast stations named after TV series) should remain in Live TV rather than be incorrectly treated as episodes.
- Browser playback requires supported streams, HTTPS, codecs, provider permissions, and eligible account entitlements.
- This software does not include film or TV programming, catalog licensing, or bundled playlist sources.
