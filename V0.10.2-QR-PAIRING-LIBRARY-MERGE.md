# TunelyTV v0.10.2 — Preserve Movies/Series + QR TV Pairing

This corrective release reconciles the QR pairing update with the subsequent v0.10.0 and v0.10.1 library work. It should be deployed INSTEAD of the older v0.9.10 QR ZIP, which was produced from an obsolete branch and displaced library behavior.

## Preserved and restored

- Authorized Movies and TV Series rails, with a separate Where to Watch discovery section.
- Per-playlist content classification: Automatic, Live TV, Movies/VOD, and TV Series/Episodes; editable in Account → Sources.
- Hidden titles with account-specific save/restore functionality and no URL or provider secrets stored in the hidden-list ledger.
- Last-viewed channel restoration when browser autoplay policy allows it (muted fallback where needed).
- M3U Plus credential-protected provider metadata reader and optional conventional XMLTV discovery.
- QR TV pairing preview at `/tv`, phone claim flow at `/activate`, short-lived pairing codes, existing account licensing and TV-slot enforcement.
- No bundled channels, movie streams, or broadcaster artwork. TMDb and external logo matching stay off without licensing permission.

## Installation and verification

1. Back up the persistent Railway volume before installing. Do **not** delete or move `/app/instance`.
2. Commit the extracted package contents at the repository ROOT; do not upload the ZIP as a single file. Keep the existing `TUNELY_FERNET_KEY` unchanged.
3. Confirm Railway serves `/`, `/account`, `/watch`, `/tv` and `/activate` and that the backend health endpoint reports `0.10.2`.
4. Under Account → Sources, verify a saved source can be changed to Movies or Series without re-uploading.
5. On Home, confirm Movies and TV Series come from your own playlists and Where to Watch remains separate.
6. Test hiding/restoring a title, last-channel selection, QR pairing from a phone, and authorized XMLTV loading on your real devices.

## Release limitations

QR pairing is a web TV simulator, not a native TV APK. Xtream playback, HTTP MPEG-TS, provider CORS, codec availability, and EPG coverage have independent limits. TMDb/JustWatch use requires appropriate licensing. No live provider credentials or application secrets are included in this package. The package is prepared but has not been deployed by ChatGPT.
