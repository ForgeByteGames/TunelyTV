EASY SETUP

Let's get connected.

Here's how the TunelyTV device and playlist setup will work.

RECOMMENDED

Pair with an in-app code

When TunelyTV launches on your TV, it will display a temporary pairing code. Sign in on this website, enter the code, then assign one of your playlists to that device.

1. Open TunelyTV on the TV.2. Note the displayed pairing code.3. Enter the code on this dashboard.4. Choose an authorized playlist.
Available now in the included TV app simulator; native apps come later

Manual MAC / Device ID

Some players and licensed provider portals identify a device with a MAC address or provider-issued Device ID. Record it here for the device profile; any provider-specific activation stays with that provider.

Hardware MAC is not available from a normal web page.

Local IP address

You may save a device's local IP as a setup note. Home IPs can change and generally cannot be used to reach a TV from an internet website or prove ownership.

Local discovery would require an opt-in local companion or app.

Bring your own playlist

Supply an M3U or M3U8 URL, upload a local M3U file, or enter the credentials for an authorized Xtream-compatible service. No content is included with TunelyTV.
